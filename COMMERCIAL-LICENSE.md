# Commercial licensing boundary

Woobe Community is intended to be useful as a real self-hosted product, not as a crippled trial.

The Community license is designed to allow companies to run Woobe internally and to build commercial products that use Agents, Agent Networks, Runtime APIs and ChatSurface. The customer's product may charge its own users.

A separate commercial/OEM agreement is intended when the commercial value being sold is substantially access to the Woobe control plane itself, including hosted Woobe, white-label control-plane offerings, resale or OEM redistribution.

Woobe Cloud is a separate managed offering that may charge for managed infrastructure, storage, backups, upgrades, support, capacity and usage according to its commercial terms.

Enterprise capabilities such as SSO/SAML, SCIM, advanced governance, advanced audit export, external KMS/secrets, supported HA and SLA are future commercial capabilities and are not prerequisites for the Community release.

This document explains product intent; the definitive legal terms are in `LICENSE.md` and any signed commercial agreement.
