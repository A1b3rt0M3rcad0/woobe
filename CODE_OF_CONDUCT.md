# Code of Conduct

Woobe expects technical collaboration to remain professional, specific and respectful.

Contributors should discuss code, architecture, product tradeoffs and evidence rather than personal characteristics. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disruption and publication of private security information are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is expected. Strong criticism of an implementation or proposal is acceptable when it is relevant and reasoned; personal attacks are not.

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these rules and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

Security reports must follow `SECURITY.md` rather than being posted publicly.
