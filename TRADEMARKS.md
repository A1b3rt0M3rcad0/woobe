# Woobe Trademark Policy

The Woobe name, logo and other distinctive brand assets identify the official Woobe product and distributions.

The software license does not grant a general trademark license. You may make accurate nominative references such as "compatible with Woobe", "built with Woobe" or "forked from Woobe" when the wording does not imply sponsorship or official status.

You may not, without written permission:

- present a fork or modified build as the official Woobe distribution;
- use Woobe branding as the primary brand of a competing hosted control plane;
- use a confusingly similar name or logo for a redistributed control plane;
- remove notices in a way that misrepresents the origin of the software.

Forks should use their own product name and visual identity while retaining attribution required by the software license.
