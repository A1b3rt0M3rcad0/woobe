The Woobe name, logos, symbols, product marks and distinctive brand assets identify Woobe and its official distributions and services.

This policy governs those assets separately from the software permissions in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and [LICENSE_EE.md](LICENSE_EE.md). Their inclusion in the repository does not place them under the general Core or Enterprise software grant.

Neither software license nor an Enterprise Production Authorization grants a general right to use the Woobe brand as the identity of a third-party business or offering, sell the Woobe platform, or claim official representation.

## 1. Ordinary identification of a legitimate installation

You may preserve and display original Woobe branding as incorporated into a lawfully obtained official distribution while using that distribution as permitted by its applicable software license.

You may retain technical identifiers needed for interoperability, including module names, imports, internal paths and the identification of a private deployment. This does not authorize claiming a public namespace, account or offering is operated by Woobe.

Copyright and license notices must be preserved as required by the applicable software license. Identifying the origin of software does not mean Woobe operates, supports or endorses your deployment.

## 2. Truthful descriptive references

You may refer to Woobe in text to accurately identify the technology, explain compatibility, attribute its source or discuss it in documentation, tutorials, research, reviews and other lawful publications.

Examples of permitted truthful references include:

- "built with Woobe";
- "compatible with Woobe";
- "integrates with Woobe";
- "forked from Woobe";
- "independent consulting for Woobe deployments".

Such references must be descriptive and secondary to your own identity. They must not suggest that your organization, software or service is official, certified, sponsored, endorsed or operated by Woobe. Compatibility and integration claims must be accurate.

Factual screenshots used to document, review or explain a legitimate installation may show its existing branding without presenting that branding as your own identity or endorsement.

Describing a service truthfully does not authorize it. Consulting must comply with the applicable software license, and a descriptive reference cannot make prohibited platform resale or paid platform access permissible.

## 3. Uses not granted

The repository licenses and this policy do not permit you to:

- use Woobe or a confusingly similar mark as the name or primary identity of your own company, platform, commercial product or service;
- use the Woobe logo or distinctive brand assets as the identity of your offering or as a badge of official approval in advertising;
- market a third-party supply of the platform as "Woobe Hosted", "Managed Woobe", "Woobe Cloud" or an equivalent paid Woobe offering;
- sell the Woobe platform under its original brand;
- claim to be Woobe, its official representative, an authorized reseller, an official partner or official support when that status has not actually been conferred by Woobe;

- use a domain, public package or container namespace, marketplace listing or social account in a way that implies official ownership, sponsorship or affiliation;
- imitate Woobe's distinctive identity to confuse users about the origin of software or services;
- remove or change legal attribution to misrepresent the origin of the software.

An Enterprise Production Authorization is evidence only of the software rights it expressly grants. It is not evidence of a trademark license, partnership, certification, reseller status or endorsement.

## 4. Forks and modified installations

Distributed forks and derivatives must identify their independent origin and modifications. Use your own identity for the fork while retaining required legal notices and truthful attribution to the original code.

You may adapt the visible interface of a permitted internal deployment or application while retaining required legal notices. Changing the interface or removing original marketing branding does not authorize commercial supply of the Woobe platform.

Ordinary technical identifiers, repository fork relationships and factual references to upstream Woobe do not by themselves claim official status. Avoid presenting a modified fork as an unmodified official release.

## 5. Independent software and trademark restrictions

Software rights, Enterprise Production Use rights and brand permissions are separate.

A different name or logo does not authorize selling or supplying paid access to the Woobe platform or its derivatives. Likewise, retaining the original name and logo does not authorize acting as a reseller.

The limited identification and descriptive-reference permissions above grant no commercial platform-supply rights and do not override either software license.
