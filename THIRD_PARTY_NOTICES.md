# Third-Party Notices

Woobe Community includes third-party software that remains governed by the original license terms of each component.

The manual `Container quality` workflow generates SPDX SBOMs for the exact API, Web and ChatSurface container artifacts and derives a `woobe-third-party-inventory` artifact from those SBOMs. That inventory records the artifact, package, version, declared license and concluded license reported for every discovered component.

Before a public release, the generated inventory and SBOMs must be reviewed against the original component licenses. Any attribution, notice text, source offer or other distribution obligation required by those licenses must be included in the release and reflected here when applicable.

`docs/PUBLIC_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md` remains the publication gate. A successful inventory generation does not by itself certify legal compliance; the exact release inventory still requires review before publication.
